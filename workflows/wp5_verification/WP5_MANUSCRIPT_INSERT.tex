\subsection{Verification, uncertainty, and conditioning}
\label{sec:verification}

Because the proposed mechanism combines a nonlinear relative equilibrium, a
matrix-free Floquet calculation, and packet-profile fitting, we performed
independent audits of each numerical layer.  For the two principal midpoint
cases, changing the block-Ritz dimension from 6 to 10 and using four independent
random seeds changes the leading spectral radius by only
$7.8\times10^{-11}$ for $(h,\kappa)=(0.01,5)$ and
$4.0\times10^{-12}$ for $(0.02,20)$.  Fixed-phase domain variations over
$14\le L\le20$ change $\rho_F$ by at most $2.6\times10^{-5}$, less than
$2\times10^{-4}$ of the corresponding instability margin $\rho_F-1$.
Even deliberately loose Newton tolerances alter $\rho_F$ by at most
$1.9\times10^{-4}$ in these tests.  Analytic tangent/adjoint implementations
satisfy discrete adjoint identities at $10^{-14}$--$10^{-15}$, while central
finite differences reach relative tangent errors of $O(10^{-5})$--$O(10^{-4})$,
consistent with the $C^1$ but non-$C^2$ regularity of $|u|u$ at zero.

The Fourier iteration for the stencil-dependent Nyquist solitary profiles was
also repeated over envelope domains of 60--120 cells and with 8192--32768
Fourier points.  The velocity coefficients $V_{i,h}$ are unchanged to
floating-point precision and the profile-equation residuals remain below
$3.0\times10^{-13}$.  Packet amplitudes and center velocities are insensitive
to the local fit window and to trimming the clean tracking interval.  We
therefore report deterministic fit-sensitivity envelopes rather than treating
the computed snapshots as statistically noisy observations.

For the revised fully discrete velocity laws, the independent relative
prediction discrepancies are $7.4\times10^{-6}$ and $5.2\times10^{-7}$ for
the two archived midpoint packets and $7.8\times10^{-8}$ and
$5.7\times10^{-8}$ for the two natural GL4 packets.  For the independent
Ismail--Taha and Pad\'e-8 tests, correcting the small amplitude mismatch between
the natural and separately prepared waves reduces their relative speed
differences to $1.2\times10^{-7}$--$2.6\times10^{-7}$.  Thus the remaining
mismatches should be viewed as small deterministic model/extraction errors,
not stochastic confidence intervals.

Finally, the Floquet eigenproblems are appreciably non-normal.  With unit
right and left eigenvectors, representative eigenvalue condition numbers are
approximately 254 for midpoint at $(h,\kappa)=(0.01,5)$, 45 for midpoint at
$(0.02,20)$, and 94 for GL4 at $(0.01,5)$.  This conditioning explains the
importance of adjoint projections and transient edge amplification, while the
positive instability margins remain orders of magnitude larger than all
solver and eigensolver uncertainties.  Full tolerance, domain, fitting,
storage-precision, and timing audits are reported in the Supplementary
Material and reproducibility archive.
